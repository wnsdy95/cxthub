# Code of conduct

We want cxthub collaboration to be technically rigorous, welcoming, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith while evidence is gathered.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and confidential security reports.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and
  publishing another person's private information.
- Stop behavior when a maintainer asks you to de-escalate.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
threads, project chat, and public events where someone represents cxthub.

## Reporting and enforcement

For confidential reports, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form
and prefix the title with `[conduct]`. Do not expose sensitive evidence in a
public issue. Maintainers will limit access to people needed for review, avoid
conflicts of interest, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban
participants depending on severity and recurrence.

Good-faith reports and participation in an investigation must not be
retaliated against. Deliberately false or abusive reports may themselves
violate this policy.
