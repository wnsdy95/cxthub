The public `src/strconv/number.go` wrapper and JSON `decode.go`/`stream.go` are
contract/API-boundary references, not a license to retain whole-token
`ParseFloat`/`Decoder` work. The parent owns integration and cooperative original
error reconstruction after semantic overflow rejection.

The adaptation must preserve signed zero, nearest/ties-to-even rounding,
subnormal/underflow behavior, exact/fast/fallback selection, and the actual
800-digit fallback operation order. Both exponent scans update only while the
accumulator is below 10000, may overshoot to 99999, and still consume subsequent
digits. Primary total-significant-digit and fallback retained-digit point rules
remain distinct; they are not interchangeable mathematical normalization.
Fixed tables and fixed arrays/scalars do not by themselves prove total retained
memory, cancellation behavior, throughput, or source-fence correctness.
